Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful Answers for Patients

Get the answers you need, and stay informed and confident at every step.

At iTrust Cares, we believe quality mental health care should be accessible and affordable. We accept an extensive array of insurance plans, including but not limited to SC Medicare, BCBS, Humana, Aetna, United Healthcare, and several commercial plans.

Don't see yours listed? Give us a call and we'll gladly check your coverage.
Upstate: 864-970-2273  |  Lowcountry: 843-285-6757

Getting started is simple, and we guide you through every step. It begins with a referral or a signed consent to treat, which can come from the resident, a family member, or the community's care team. From there, our intake team coordinates directly with your community, so families never have to chase paperwork.

One of our providers then meets the resident on-site for an initial consultation and builds a personalized care plan from that first visit. You will always know who your provider is and what comes next.

The first visit is an unhurried conversation, not a rushed exam. Our provider meets the resident in the comfort of their own community, learns their history and current concerns, and listens to what matters most to them and their family. There is nothing to prepare and nowhere to travel.

By the end, we lay out a clear, personalized plan in plain language and explain the next steps.

All care is delivered on-site at the senior living communities we partner with across the Upstate and Lowcountry. Residents are seen in the familiar comfort of their own home, with no travel, no waiting rooms, and no disruption to their routine.

If your community is not a partner yet, reach out and we will talk with your team about bringing our services on-site.

Communication is part of the care. Our providers coordinate directly with your community's team about how each resident is doing, and we keep families in the loop on the plan and any changes, so no one is left guessing.

Between visits, you can reach our office by call or text in the Upstate at 864-970-2273 or the Lowcountry at 843-285-6757, or email clinical@itrustcares.com, and a member of our team will help.

Our nurse practitioners and physician assistants specialize in the emotional and cognitive well-being of older adults. We support residents navigating depression, anxiety, grief and loss, the adjustment to community living, and cognitive changes such as those related to dementia.

Several of our providers are Certified Dementia Practitioners, and every visit is centered on the person, not just a diagnosis.
